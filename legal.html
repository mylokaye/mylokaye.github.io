Dynamics 365 Form Debugger privacy

Dynamics 365 Form Debugger is a dependency-free Chrome and Microsoft Edge extension for testing Dynamics 365 Customer Insights - Journeys forms. The extension does not collect, store, transmit, or share form values, browsing history, credentials, or telemetry.

What the extension does

  • Applies #d365mkt-nocache on supported Dynamics asset URLs while extension features are enabled.
  • Reads the active Dynamics Form ID from the current page and shows it only in the popup.
  • Renders editable debug copies of hidden Dynamics fields while keeping the original controls in place.
  • Synchronises edits to the original control so the host form can submit them through its normal process.

Local storage

The extension stores only its enabled or disabled preference through chrome.storage.local. It does not persist form IDs, form values, page content, or submission data.

Permissions and site access

  • activeTab allows the popup to request the detected Form ID from the current tab.
  • storage remembers whether extension features are enabled.
  • *://*.dynamics.com/* access allows supported Dynamics asset URLs to receive cache bypass.
  • <all_urls> content-script access allows detection when Dynamics forms are embedded on third-party websites. Inspection is limited to detected Dynamics form containers.

Edited values may be transmitted by the website hosting the form only if you choose to submit that form. That submission is controlled by the host website, not by this extension.

Stores, source, and support

Install the extension from the Chrome Web Store or Microsoft Edge Add-ons. Its source and change history are available in the Form-Debugger-D365 repository.

Manchester Tram Times privacy

Manchester Tram Times lets you check real-time Manchester Metrolink tram departures across all 99 stations, view disruptions, and check how busy your next tram is expected to be.

Personal information and sign-in

Manchester Tram Times has no account or sign-in feature. The app does not ask you for a name, email address, phone number, precise location, or other personal information, and it does not create user profiles or keep a visitor search history. It requests current tram service data so it can display departures, disruptions, and crowding information; it does not request your device location.

The app may save non-personal interface preferences in your browser. These are not linked to an account or sent as personal details. You can remove them by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Service providers and recipients

The published app is hosted through OpenAI's ChatGPT Sites service and delivered with Cloudflare. Those providers may process technical connection data such as your IP address, browser or device information, request details, and security data to host, deliver, and protect the site. Cloudflare may set its __cf_bm security cookie; Cloudflare says it expires after 30 minutes of continuous inactivity. See OpenAI's ChatGPT Sites terms, OpenAI's ChatGPT Sites data processing addendum, Cloudflare's privacy policy, and Cloudflare's cookie information.

To provide live tram information, the app's service requests operational data from Transport for Greater Manchester (TfGM). These requests are made by the service, and the app does not send visitor names, contact details, or device-location data to TfGM. The app does not sell personal information or use it for advertising.

Retention and your controls

Manchester Tram Times does not retain personal information or maintain a visitor history. Interface preferences remain in your browser until you clear the site's data. The Cloudflare security cookie expires after 30 minutes of continuous inactivity. OpenAI and Cloudflare set their own retention periods for technical connection and security records under their policies and applicable legal requirements; Manchester Tram Times does not set those provider retention periods.

You can use the app without signing in, avoid providing personal information, and clear browser site data to remove local preferences and cookies. If you email for support, your email address and message are used to respond to you and handled by the email providers involved; you can ask for the support correspondence to be deleted using the contact details at the bottom of this page. It is kept only as long as needed to handle the request and any necessary follow-up or legal recordkeeping.
